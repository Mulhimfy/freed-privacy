How to play

Drag one finger to dig a tunnel through the earth. Reach the prisoners' cell, break them out and lead them back to the surface. Each prison adds a new hurdle: guards, laser grids, water, lava that cools into obsidian, keys and iron gates, and explosives. The in-game How to play guide (Settings) explains every one.

Questions

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap Show me how on the level screen to watch the solution. It is available on most levels in every prison. You can also hold the rewind button to undo your last moves.

How do I unlock new gear?

Earn stars by finishing levels well, then open the Locker to try on and equip new suits and lamps.

Will I lose my progress if I update the game?

No. Updates keep your progress. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it, because progress is stored only on your device.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open Settings in the game, or your phone's notification settings for FREED.

Does FREED need an internet connection?

No. The whole game plays offline.
